## Back2Basics: PCB Doodles

> * Yuri makes random PCBs to take a break from the absolute insanity they've been doing lately.

### About The Project

Welcome to **Back2Basics / PCB Doodles**—a therapeutic departure from dense, high-density polygon meat-grinders and hyper-complex hardware engineering. This project is about scaling back, returning to fundamental analog concepts, and making weird, standalone electronic art just for the sake of it.

The goal of this project is to go into random spaces in or out of the city and just make stuff. Just do things. It doesn't have to be consistent or even useful—just *things*.

Originally planned as a 10-part series, part one got made out of Hackatime (which is kinda sorta chaotic), meaning math dictates that there are now actually **11** parts to do. This is a proud **Stardance** project hopefully funded by the legends at Hack Club.

---

### The First Doodle: Triple-555 RGB Color-Shifter

The inaugural PCB doodle strips electronics down to its roots:

* **Core Logic:** Three separate astable oscillators powered by triple **ICM7555xB CMOS timers** chosen for low power, zero decoupling requirements, and high impedance.
* **The Visuals:** Wired to drift wildly out of sync, driving a **Common Cathode RGB LED** through a cascading sea of carefully tuned resistors and capacitors.
* **Power:** Runs smoothly off a single **3V coin cell battery** (complete with critical silkscreen instructions: *"yes, this is where the battery goes. good boy/girl/they/whatever you go by"*).
